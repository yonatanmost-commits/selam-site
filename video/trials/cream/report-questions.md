# Builder's report: questions

Asked by the planner session at the user's request. The answers feed a long-term
video-editing skill, so **every answer has two parts**:

- **This run:** what was true only for the cream video.
- **Carries forward:** the workflow, pattern or fix that would hold for any of Selam's videos.

Write the answers to `video/trials/cream/report.md`, under the same numbers. Say "don't know"
or "not reached" where that is the truth. Give commands, settings and numbers where you have
them. This repo is public: nothing private about Selam beyond what the log already holds.

## Workflow
1. What were the real steps, in the order they actually happened, and where did that order differ from the plan?
2. Which steps needed a person, how long did each wait take, and which waits could be batched into one sitting with Selam?
3. What is the shortest path from "clips in a folder" to "draft she can watch", now that it has been done once?

## Transcription
4. Which speech engine and model ended up working on this PC, how long did it take per minute of audio, and what failed first?
5. What kinds of words did it get wrong in her Hebrew (ingredient names, brand words, fillers), and is there a correction list worth carrying forward?
6. How was the transcript shown to Selam for correction, and what would make that faster for her?

## Choosing the cut
7. How did you decide which segments to keep: what did you look at, at what frame density, and what did that cost in plan usage?
8. What makes a good first three seconds in her footage, as a rule that applies to the next video?
9. How did you line up the raw clips with the CapCut export, and is that step needed at all when starting from raw clips only?

## Rendering
10. What did the edit file end up containing that the plan did not foresee, and what would you remove from it?
11. Which ffmpeg problems cost the most time (rotation, HEVC, Hebrew letter order, fonts, punctuation, timing drift), and what is the fix for each?
12. What caption settings worked: font, size, position, line length, time on screen? Which are her taste and which are TikTok's layout?
13. What audio chain produced the loudness target, and how was the music ducked under her voice?

## Working with Selam
14. What did her notes look like in her own words, and how did each translate into an edit-file change? Which notes could not be done, and why?
15. What did she care about that nobody predicted?

## Music and TikTok
16. What did the account check show about music, where did the track come from, and what is the repeatable way to pick and record a licensed track?

## The spikes
17. For the MCP server and the CapCut draft route: what worked, what broke, and under what conditions would either be the better choice?

## For the skill itself
18. If a fresh session had to edit her next video with no memory of this one, what three things would it get wrong without being told?
19. What would Selam need to type or say to start a video on her own laptop, and what would stop her?
20. What in the scripts is hard-coded to this video and has to be lifted out before they serve the next one?
21. What did the trial cost in minutes for her, for you and for the planner, and which step is the next bottleneck?
