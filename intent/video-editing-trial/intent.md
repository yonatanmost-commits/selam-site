# Intent: Video editing trial for Selam's TikTok
Author: Yonatan, on behalf of Selam (owner, סלם · רפואת שורש). Date: 2026-10-11.
Source: idea. First lane of a wider plan to grow this repo beyond the website.

## Problem
- Selam's TikTok is working: orders are waiting for soaps to cure, and the 118-bar order is packed to go out around Tuesday.
- Video editing is the major marketing bottleneck. One video costs her a few days of real, hands-on work.
- She edits alone, on her phone, in CapCut.
- All four parts of the edit hurt: cutting and trimming, Hebrew captions and on-screen text, music and pacing, and look and polish.
- Her videos mix process footage, Selam talking to camera in Hebrew, and voiceover on footage.
- Nobody here knows yet whether Claude Code (through MCP or any other method) can take real editing work off her. That is why the research comes before any commitment.

## Proposed outcome
- One current video is edited as a trial and Selam posts it on TikTok, because it is good enough by her own taste.
- The trial video is the cream-making video (currently a 2:57 CapCut cut). It comes out tighter, with Hebrew captions and on-screen text, with cleaned-up sound, and with music.
- Selam wants it viral and appealing to women. The edit is made to travel on TikTok and to speak to women, who are her audience and her clients.
- Selam keeps the taste. She says what she wants in plain words, sees a draft and gives notes. Only the mechanics are taken off her.
- A short written recommendation exists: the methods that were tried or considered, compared, and one recommended way of working that Selam could run herself.

## Affected users and systems
- Selam: films, directs, approves, and is meant to run the workflow herself afterwards, on a computer she would use.
- Yonatan: runs the trial on this PC and sets it up.
- Her TikTok account, where the result is published. Customers arrive from there to the site.
- The trial footage: `Videos/WhatsApp Video 2026-10-11 at 20.38.57.mp4` is a 478×850 WhatsApp-compressed copy with a CapCut watermark. The full-quality originals are on her phone.
- This repo, which is public and serves the live site.

## Constraints
- Free or already-owned tools only. No new paid subscriptions and no per-video costs. The Claude subscription itself is the one accepted cost (assumed, to be confirmed in review).
- Footage stays local. Frames and transcript may go to Claude so it can judge cuts and write captions. No other third-party service receives the footage.
- No AI-generated voice or imagery. Only her real footage and her real voice.
- Music must come from a free, properly licensed source.
- The trial works from full-quality originals taken off her phone, not from the WhatsApp copy.
- Footage is never committed to this repo, because the repo is public.
- The result must fit her brand: professional, slow-craft, never childish.
- Captions and on-screen text are in Hebrew, in her own words and her own spellings.
- The recommended way of working must be one Selam can run without Yonatan.

## Out of scope
- Managing production, the R&D and scientific recipe database, and business management. Each is a later lane of its own.
- Choosing the account or plan for Selam (Pro, Cowork or otherwise).
- Posting to TikTok, scheduling, and content strategy. Selam publishes herself.
- Editing more than one video.
- Any change to the live website.

## Open questions
- How will the originals get off her phone at full quality, and are the raw clips still there or only the CapCut export?
- Which computer will Selam use, and is it capable enough for video work?
- How much of her editing time does the trial actually save? Nobody has measured the "few days" in hours.
- What does "viral" mean for her in numbers (views, shares, orders), and against which of her earlier videos is it compared? No edit can guarantee it.
- Where does "viral" pull against "professional, slow-craft, never childish", and which wins when it does?
- How long should the finished video be? "Tighter" has no number yet.
- Can a method that satisfies "free" and "footage stays local" also be simple enough for her to run alone, or do those pull against each other?
- Is a free, properly licensed track good enough for her, compared with the sounds available inside TikTok?
